safety covers how training goes wrong and how to fix it. most lessons reuse the grpo and rlvr code from training/rl and a small llm judge.

reward hacking happens when rl learns to game the verifier instead of solving the task. emergent misalignment shows that narrow finetuning on bad behaviour can make a model broadly misaligned, and inoculation prompting prevents it. harmful finetuning shows how a few hundred examples remove safety training, unlearning shows that removed knowledge comes back easily, and character training is the modern form of constitutional ai.
